# INCOIS advisories — what they are
Source: INCOIS PFZ advisory pages https://www.incois.gov.in/MarineFisheries/PfzAdvisory
and Ocean State Forecast https://incois.gov.in/oceanservices/osfforecast.jsp
(verified live 2026-09-28; HTML pages, no open JSON API).

- Potential Fishing Zone (PFZ) advisories are issued daily per coastal sector,
  derived from satellite SST and chlorophyll. Valid roughly 24 hours.
- Ocean State Forecasts cover waves, wind, currents and SST for Indian waters.
- Fish-finding zones shift with ocean dynamics; treat advisory positions as
  approximate, not exact coordinates.
