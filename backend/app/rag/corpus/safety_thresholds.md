# Safety thresholds — provisional ORCA rules
Source: ORCA Phase 3 design (pending domain review). Wind limits follow the
Beaufort scale bands used in IMD-style marine advisories (see IMD Mausam
https://mausam.imd.gov.in/ and RSMC https://rsmcnewdelhi.imd.gov.in/).

- Wind above 40 km/h: caution. Small craft feel strong breeze effects.
- Wind above 61 km/h: unsafe. Near-gale conditions.
- Wave height above 2.5 m: caution for small fishing vessels.
- Wave height above 4.0 m: unsafe.
- Any active IMD district warning: minimum verdict is caution.
