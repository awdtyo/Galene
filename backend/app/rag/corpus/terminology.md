# Marine terminology — exact expansions only
Source: ORCA glossary, cross-checked against INCOIS
https://www.incois.gov.in/MarineFisheries/PfzAdvisory and MarineRegions
https://www.marineregions.org/ (verified 2026-09-28).

- PFZ = Potential Fishing Zone. Never "Protected", "Private", or any other
  expansion. PFZ advisories mark probable fish-aggregation areas from
  satellite SST and chlorophyll.
- OSF = Ocean State Forecast (INCOIS): waves, wind, currents, SST.
- EEZ = Exclusive Economic Zone (200 nautical miles).
- WDPA = World Database on Protected Areas. MPA = Marine Protected Area.
- IMD = India Meteorological Department. RSMC = Regional Specialised
  Meteorological Centre (New Delhi, cyclones).
- SST in degrees Celsius (C), wind in km/h, wave height in metres (m),
  distances in km or nautical miles (NM) labelled explicitly.
