# Geofencing disclaimer — boundaries are simplified
Sources: MarineRegions EEZ v12 (CC-BY, doi:10.14284/632,
https://geo.vliz.be/geoserver/MarineRegions/wfs) and WDPA via public ArcGIS
REST (https://data-gis.unep-wcmc.org/server/rest/services/ProtectedPlanet/WDPCA/FeatureServer).

- ORCA ships SIMPLIFIED polygons (EEZ tolerance 0.02 deg; 6 WDPA India marine
  sites). They are sufficient for demo geofence warnings, NOT for navigation
  or legal boundary decisions.
- Fishermen must respect official charts, MPA regulations, and international
  maritime boundaries issued by competent authorities.
